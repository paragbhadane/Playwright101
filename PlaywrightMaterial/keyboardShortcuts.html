Vidyarthi Panch Lakshnam - Chanakya Niti

Source

Kaak Cheshta: Maintain relentless curiosity and persistent effort to seek knowledge just like a crow.

Bako Dhyanam: Concentrate single-mindedly on your goal, just like a crane waiting for its prey.

Shwan Nidra: Be watchful of your responsibilities, sleep less and stay alert just like a dog.

Alpahaari: Eat light to keep the body active, agile and free from lethargy.

Griha Tyagi: Step out of your comfort zone and home luxuries for the sake of education.
